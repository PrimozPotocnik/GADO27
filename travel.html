How to get there

Distances and travel times are approximate. Details will be checked before the conference.

Venue

The conference takes place at Hotel Belvedere in Izola, on a hill above the town with views over the Gulf of Trieste. Izola is on the Slovenian coast of the Adriatic Sea, a few kilometres from Koper and about 20 km from Trieste, Italy.

By air

  • Trieste Airport (TRS): the nearest major airport, about 50 km away. Onward travel is by shuttle, train and bus, or taxi.
  • Ljubljana Airport (LJU): about 100 km away. A shuttle or bus to Ljubljana, followed by a bus to Izola, is the usual route.
  • Venice Marco Polo (VCE): about 180 km away. Reach Trieste by train or bus, then continue to Izola.

By train and bus

Trains run to Ljubljana and to Trieste. From Ljubljana, buses to Koper and Izola run several times a day, and the journey takes about two hours. From Trieste, regional buses run to Koper and Izola. From the Izola bus station, a taxi to the hotel takes a few minutes.

By car

Izola is reached from Ljubljana via the A1 motorway towards Koper, in about 1 hour 15 minutes. Motorway vignettes are required in Slovenia. Parking is available at the hotel.

Arrival

Registration starts on Monday 19 April at 15:00 at the hotel reception. If you plan to arrive late, please let us know in advance.
